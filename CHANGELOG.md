# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-22

- First public source and downloadable release.
- Universal Mac app containing Apple silicon and Intel code, targeting macOS 13 or later. Intel hardware testing remains outstanding.
- Public installation, compatibility, snapshot and contribution documentation.
- Synthetic regression examples suitable for public distribution.
- Release remains ad-hoc signed and not notarized.

## 1.0.2 — 2026-10-19

- Corrected recovered subtitle fill by writing a negative stroke width, verified against a controlled Final Cut Face on/off export and a fresh import.
- Preserved editable text, timing, outline and shadow.

## 1.0.1 — 2026-10-14

- Combined exact reciprocal linked picture/audio pairs into one AV placement to prevent duplicate clips after import.
- Used explicit video/audio components for independent edits.
- Corrected source-relative volume keyframe timing and finite zero-gain formatting.
- Preserved picture when muting the audio of a combined clip.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-14

- Initial local Mac app and CLI for editable XML conversion, media relinking, reports and optional text recovery.
